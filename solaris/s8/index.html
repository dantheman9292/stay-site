The home

Solaris is a seven-bedroom home in Racquet Club West, 6 minutes from downtown Palm Springs. Five suites are in the main house, with two more in the detached casita.

Spend time by the private courtyard pool and gold-tile hot tub, or gather around the fire pit. A covered outdoor kitchen and dining area face the mountains. Inside, the vaulted great room opens to a chef's kitchen with two fridges, two ovens and two dishwashers.
