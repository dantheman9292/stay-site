The home

Solaris is a seven-bedroom home in Racquet Club West, 6 minutes from downtown Palm Springs. Five suites are in the main house, with two more in the detached casita.

Spend time by the private courtyard pool and gold-tile hot tub, or gather around the fire pit. The outdoor kitchen and dining area face the mountains. Inside, the vaulted great room opens to a chef's kitchen with two fridges, two ovens and two dishwashers.

Bedrooms

6 king beds and 2 twin XL beds (the twins can be made up as a king for a fee), every bedroom with its own bathroom; 5 suites in the main house and 2 in the detached casita
