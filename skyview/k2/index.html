The home

Skyview brings contemporary interiors and mountain views together in Palm Springs. Retracting glass doors open the main living area to the outdoors, while an equipped kitchen and spacious dining area make it easy to gather for meals.

Head outside for the pool, hot tub, barbecue or a round of lawn games, then settle into a hammock or beside the fire pit. Indoors, a dedicated game room offers billiards, air hockey and an arcade game, and the gym has a Peloton bike and strength equipment.

Bedrooms

One king bed in each of the four bedrooms. Bedrooms 1 and 4 have en suite bathrooms; bedrooms 2 and 3 share a hallway bathroom. An air mattress can be requested.
